# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Japanese women in their 20s–30s who live active, high-perspiration lives: nurses pulling long ward shifts, field sales reps doing outdoor rounds, athletes and recreational runners. Their shared wound: they have quietly accepted that sunscreen washes off when they sweat, and stopped expecting otherwise. They are educated consumers who distrust hype and respond to specificity, proof, and confidence.

## Product Purpose

UV FORTE シールドセラム is a premium SPF50+ PA++++ serum from LUMIÉRE that strengthens its UV barrier on contact with sweat, thanks to a patented ActiveShield formulation (特許第1234567号). The LP's job is to convert first-time visitors into subscription purchasers by making the patent technology credible, the daily benefit vivid, and the 30-day money-back guarantee feel risk-free.

## Brand Personality

研ぎ澄まされた · 美しい · 強い  
(Refined precision · Quiet beauty · Physical strength)

Voice: confident without shouting. Writes like a trusted expert, not a salesperson. Uses the language of the user's daily life (the ward, the commute, the track) to ground every claim.

## Anti-references

- Busy drugstore promotional material: bright oranges, pop typography, discount stickers, loud urgency.
- Clinical pharma aesthetics: white backgrounds, cold grid tables, ingredient lists as the main event, no warmth.
- Generic prestige-beauty LP: generic gold gradients, rose-gold accents, template "luxury" that could belong to any cosmetic brand.

## Design Principles

1. **Proof over promise.** Every benefit claim must be anchored to a specific mechanism, number, or real voice. Abstractions earn no trust here.
2. **The body is the brief.** The target user is physical, sweating, in motion. The design should feel like it was made for someone who is outside, not browsing a boutique.
3. **Confidence without bravado.** Strong hierarchy and decisive color use. Never tentative. Never shouty.
4. **Brand-specific, not category-generic.** Details that only LUMIÉRE could say (patent number, co-development with 15 dermatologists, 78% reorder rate) are foregrounded over generic beauty LP scaffolding.
5. **Earned premium.** Pricing and authority signals land because the preceding sections built credibility, not because of decorative gold.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA minimum. Body text ≥4.5:1 contrast against its background. Interactive elements ≥3:1. Reduced-motion alternative for all scroll-triggered animations. Semantic HTML structure throughout.
